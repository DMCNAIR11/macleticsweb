Product summary

Mac's Sports Playground, in brief

Mac's Sports Playground is a sports prediction and analysis app for the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college sports and soccer. Users write the recipe their picks must match, backtest it against past seasons, and let a "locker" surface matching games every day. An AI Buddy answers questions from real data.

It is a prediction and analysis platform. Ages 18+. Published by Macletics, a division of Mind of Mart LLC.
