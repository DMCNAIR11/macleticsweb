Product summary

Mac's Sports Playground, in brief

Mac's Sports Playground is a sports prediction and analysis app for NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and college sports, with soccer coming soon. Users write the recipe their picks must match, backtest it against past seasons, and let a "locker" surface matching games every day. An AI Buddy answers questions from real data.

It is a prediction and analysis platform, not a gambling app. Ages 18+.
