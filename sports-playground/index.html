What it does

Three things, done properly

The full feature list, the four membership levels and the add-on packs are on macletics.com, the product's own site.

Pick lockers & backtesting

Stack conditions on season-tagged stats, then backtest the recipe against real past seasons: record, win rate, ROI and a full game log, before it ever makes a pick. Once you trust it, the locker scans every slate for games that match.

An AI Buddy that answers from data

Ask who's hot, who's hurt or who led the league last week. Pick a personality, and get a daily brief on your teams, your picks and today's slate.

Scores, stats, odds and reports

Live scores and box scores, matchup previews, injuries, trend charts, prediction-market prices, and plain-language reports built fresh every night.
